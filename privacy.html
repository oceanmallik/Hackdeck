Privacy Policy

Effective: November 2026 • Last Updated: November 23, 2026
Summary: Hackdeck is an open-source collaborative workspace ("A Notepad for Devs"). We do not sell your personal data, run advertising networks, or monetize your private project drafts. Your data is strictly utilized to enable live collaboration, team chat, sprint task tracking, and countdown deadlines.

01 Introduction & Overview

Welcome to Hackdeck ("we", "our", or "the Platform"), accessible at hackdeck.devs.surf and developed as an open source project hosted on GitHub at oceanmallik/Hackdeck.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you access or use Hackdeck. By creating an account, authenticating via Google OAuth, or using any part of the service, you acknowledge that you have read and understood the practices described herein.

02 Information We Collect

We collect only the minimum amount of information necessary to deliver a fast, collaborative workspace experience:

  • Account Credentials (Google OAuth): When you sign in with Google, we receive basic authentication details provided by Google Identity Services, including your unique Google User ID, verified email address, display name, and avatar profile image URL. We never receive or store your Google password.
  • User Profile Information: During onboarding, you may specify a custom username/handle, display name, and skill tags (e.g. Frontend, Backend, UI/UX, AI/ML) to represent you on project boards and team rosters.
  • Workspace & Board Content: Hackathon boards created by you, including titles, external event URLs (such as Devpost or DoraHacks), submission deadlines, project drafts, architecture diagrams, GitHub repository links, and live demo endpoints.
  • Kanban Tasks & Team Messages: Task titles, descriptions, assignees, column statuses (To Do, In Progress, Done), timestamps, and team chat messages exchanged in the private team room.
  • Technical Logs: Minimal standard connection metadata (browser type, IP address, request timestamps) temporarily logged by infrastructure providers for security and diagnostic purposes.

03 How We Use Your Information

The information we collect is used strictly for operational purposes:

  • To authenticate your identity and protect workspace access.
  • To deliver real-time collaboration features (live chat message synchronization, sprint task updates, and teammate presence).
  • To compute accurate countdown timers against hackathon submission deadlines.
  • To route and process board invitations between developers.
  • To maintain system stability, diagnose technical errors, and defend against malicious attacks or spam.

We never sell, rent, or trade your personal data or project ideas to third-party data brokers or advertisers.

04 Data Storage, Security & Supabase

Hackdeck utilizes Supabase (an open-source Firebase alternative powered by PostgreSQL) for authentication, database storage, and real-time WebSocket broadcasting.

  • Row Level Security (RLS): Access to hackathon boards, tasks, invitations, and chat records is enforced at the database level using PostgreSQL Row Level Security policies to guarantee that private boards and messages are accessible only to authorized team members.
  • Encryption in Transit: All data transmissions between your browser, our client application, and backend endpoints are secured via industry-standard Transport Layer Security (TLS/HTTPS).
  • Access Control: Only team leaders have permissions to delete boards or manage member roles.

05 Your Rights & Data Control

We believe developers should retain complete control over their content:

  • Edit & Delete Chat Messages: You can edit or delete your sent messages directly within the team room at any time.
  • Task & Blueprint Management: All tasks, sprint boards, and project showcase links can be edited or deleted inline by workspace contributors.
  • Board Deletion: Team leaders can permanently delete a hackathon board, which cascades to delete all associated sprint tasks, chat history, and member links.
  • Account Deletion: You may request complete erasure of your profile and personal data by submitting an issue or contacting the project maintainer.

06 Cookies and Local Storage

Hackdeck does not use invasive advertising or third-party tracking cookies. We utilize browser localStorage solely to retain your active authentication session tokens and client preferences across page reloads.

07 Open Source Transparency

The client code of Hackdeck is open source under the MIT License and publicly inspectable at github.com/oceanmallik/Hackdeck. You can inspect every line of front-end logic, database queries, and data handling procedures directly on GitHub.
